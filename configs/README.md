# Training configurations

[narrow.json](narrow.json) trains independent samples at Re 1000 with widths 32, 64, 64. [joint.json](joint.json) trains samples and steps at alternating Re 1000 and 1580 with widths 64, 128, 128. Both read raw reference `stats.pt` files generated by [the reproduction commands](../REPRODUCE.md#regenerate-the-reference-data); the small summary archives are for analysis, not a substitute for those training inputs.

Run `python -m turb.train configs/joint.json` from the repository root. Outputs go to the configuration's `out` directory. `steps` records the final iteration index, `solver_steps_per_output * solver_dt` sets the training step interval, and `inner` sets the internal/output grid ratio. `re_ref` is a conditioning normalisation constant, not a training or evaluation Reynolds number.

`resid_switch_at` selects the split-batch statistic stage, `decay_at` reduces the learning rate, and `model_pool` retains generated training states. The measured rollouts use the saved weights without these training pools. See [the package guide](../turb/) for the architecture and losses.

The supplied weights trained with these settings approximate the [compared statistics](../data/summaries/) but miss the solver's coherent vortices and filaments in the [field examples](../data/fields/). The objectives and bounded rollouts do not establish realistic spatial organisation.
